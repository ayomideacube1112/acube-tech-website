Yes. You can contact A'Cube Tech for assistance with admission-related services for different tertiary institutions. Availability depends on the specific institution and service.

Do I need to pay before I know the price?

No. For the website's request flow, you first tell us what you need. We discuss the current price and requirements before you proceed.
